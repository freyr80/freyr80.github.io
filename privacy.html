Privacy Policy

openclaw-gog is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Gmail account, to read messages and apply labels.
  • Data is processed and stored only on the owner's private server.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
